Trade exhibitors The business halls — publishing, dev services, platforms. Trade & media badge only

Halls 2–4 are gamescom's business area: around 800 exhibitors doing the industry's trading — publishers taking pitches, outsourcing and localisation studios, engines and platforms, national pavilions and associations. It runs Wednesday to Friday and is closed at the weekend.

A trade or media badge opens it. A consumer ticket does not, so it stays off by default — turn it on and these booths become saveable, plannable stops like any other, with their own list below and their own cards in the grid above.

When to visit what

Rough strategy based on expected crowd levels. High-demand booths (queue index 4–5) mean long waits — hit those first thing in the morning or accept the queue. Calm booths are good afternoon fillers.
