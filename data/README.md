# Licensing for `data/`

The code in this repository is MIT (see the top-level `LICENSE`). The contents
of this directory are not, because they are not all the same kind of thing, and
one licence over the pile would say something untrue about most of it.

## What is licensed here

**CC BY 4.0** (`data/LICENSE`) covers the editorial work in these files:

- the selection — which exhibitors and which games are in the guide at all
- the `confirmed` / `expected` / `rumored` status calls
- crowd forecasts, queue advice, tags and categories
- descriptions, notes and the freshness annotations
- the source list and last-checked date on each entry
- the compilation itself: the structure, the joins, and the database that
  results from gathering, checking and maintaining all of the above

That last point is the substantive one. Assembling this took investment in
*obtaining, verifying and presenting* the contents — not in inventing them —
which is the kind of investment the EU database right (Directive 96/9/EC,
in Germany §§ 87a–87e UrhG) exists to protect. CC BY **4.0** is used
deliberately: it is the first version to license sui generis database rights
alongside copyright, in its Section 4. Version 3.0 would leave exactly the
right that matters here ungranted.

## What is not licensed here, by anyone

A Creative Commons licence only ever grants rights the licensor actually holds,
and there is a great deal in these files that nobody here holds:

- **Facts.** That a given exhibitor is in a given hall at a given booth number
  is a fact. Facts are not owned, not by Koelnmesse and not by this project.
  The licence above says nothing about them.
- **`hallplan/*.json`.** These booth outlines are generated by
  `tools/fetch-hallplan.mjs` from Koelnmesse's published hall-plan data (the
  source URL and fetch date are recorded in `hallplan/index.json`). They are
  reproduced here so an unofficial guide can draw a map. They are not this
  project's to relicense, and the CC BY grant does not reach them.
- **Names, logos and trade marks.** Exhibitor names, game titles and marks
  belong to their owners and appear for identification only.
- **Announcements and descriptions traceable to their source.** Where a line
  paraphrases an exhibitor's own wording, the underlying material stays theirs.

## Attributing this

Credit *gamescom.guide* with a link, and say when you took the data — it goes
stale fast, and a copy dated to the week it was taken is more honest to your
own readers than one that looks current forever. The revision it came from is
in `meta.json`.

## Why bother

The guide is free, unofficial and has nothing to sell, so none of this is about
control. It is about the repository not claiming more than it can give: a
blanket MIT over this directory would have offered strangers the right to
sublicense Koelnmesse's hall plans, which was never mine to offer.
